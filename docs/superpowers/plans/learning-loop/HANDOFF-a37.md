# HANDOFF-a37 — mc-reason-options

Written by the fix lane `fix/mc-reason-options` (spec §13 A37, a PKG-04 reopen) for its round 4: the series coordinator's design decision that ends the oscillation between two fix rounds. Rounds 1–3 are recorded in HANDOFF-04 (Post-hoc changes) and spec §13 A37; this file is the hand-off for the lane as it stands after round 4, its two reviews, the coordinator's ruling on the `mc_reason` top-up (option (c), 2026-09-28), and the review of that ruling. Read by PKG-07 (it depends on this lane), PKG-10 (misconceptions read `common_wrong_json`) and PKG-13/14. Keep every heading, even if the answer is "none".

## What changed

An `mc_reason` draft's distractors each carry their own misconception, and code derives the item's common wrong reasons from the options, so there is nothing left to cross-reference. `OptionDraft` is `{text, is_correct, misconception_key, misconception_text}`: the correct option carries no misconception; each distractor carries a snake_case key and one sentence naming the mistake that makes it tempting. `checks.common_wrong(draft)` is the item's stored `common_wrong_json`: for `mc_reason`, the distractors' misconceptions in the order written (the draft's own `wrong_keys` / `wrong_texts` are never read); for free and teachback, the paired lists as before. `lettered_options` stores each distractor's `misconception_key` as its `wrong_key`, so the stored option keys and the stored wrong reasons are one list by construction. The stored shape is unchanged, so the grader, `agents/tools/check.py`, `services/decisions.py` and PKG-10 read it as before.

Distinct keys are enforced by the schema descriptions and by validation. The rule words `correct_misconception`, `misconception_key` and `misconception_text` replace `correct_key` and `distractor_key`. Code repairs only where no guess is needed. When the correct option carries a misconception, it is cleared. When two distractors stating different misconceptions share a key, or a distractor that states one gives no key, each such distractor is keyed from its own text (`checks.misconception_slug`), with a digest appended on a clash. Two distractors stating the same misconception are rejected, whatever their keys.

The prompt, the schema descriptions, the E2E handler constants, the `WrongReasonCount` evaluator and the schema-budget pin follow. The free and teachback format lines now restate the short closing sentence, because the structure change alone tripled the direct CS101 calls (8 of 44 to 24 of 44) that left "Final answer:" off every free and teachback reference (Deviations). The check_items eval is re-recorded.

Review of round 4 (commits 88f0973, 62771ab, f5682c3). Keys have one form, `checks.wrong_key_form`: snake_case, with apostrophes dropped, accents folded, case folded, and each run of other characters written as one `_`. Validation compares keys in that form, so "Foo", "foo" and "foo " are one shared key, no longer three. It also names a key not written in that form, under `misconception_key` (or `wrong_keys` for free and teachback). `repair_draft` writes each distractor key and each free or teachback wrong key in that form. A distractor key whose form another distractor's key also has goes through the existing re-key from its own text. A derived key keeps its negations (`_NEGATIONS`), so "does not need" no longer turns into `…_need_…`. A `misconception_text` with no letter or digit, or a placeholder such as "N/A" or "None" (`_NO_MISCONCEPTION`), states no misconception and drops the draft. The replay eval is unchanged.

Review of that review (commits 6e199d2, 736a579). `wrong_key_form` keeps only Latin letters and digits, so f5682c3 had made a free or teachback key with none ("误解", "заблуждение") drop as a "blank wrong key", and two keys that differ only in other letters ("uses_λ_for_f", "uses_ν_for_f") drop as "duplicate wrong keys". The merge-base stored both. An `mc_reason` distractor with such a key was already keyed from its own text. The key-from-text repair is now one helper for both formats, `checks._keys_from_texts`. A key whose form is blank or shared is keyed from its own text (`misconception_slug`, with a digest on a clash) when every text states a misconception and no two state the same one. For free and teachback, the two lists must also be paired. So "Foo"/"foo" free keys over two different wrong texts are now re-keyed, as their distractor twins were, not dropped. A key with no Latin letter or digit is now named as such in its reason, not called "blank", and a repair line names the key it replaced, not "no key". A free or teachback wrong text that states no misconception ("N/A", "", "   ") now drops the draft under `wrong_texts`, as a placeholder `misconception_text` does. None of this changes a recorded verdict. Replaying the 288 recorded drafts (the 144 round-4 drafts in `scratchpad/mcfix5/raw_new.json` and the 144 round-3 drafts) through `repair_draft` + `validate_draft` changed 0 verdicts at d28fe4a → 736a579. Across the lane's scratch records (2051 free and teachback drafts and 4137 keys, repeats across files included), no key has a blank or shared form and no wrong text is a placeholder.

Coordinator's ruling: the `mc_reason` top-up (commits 8ea9042, ff6fd3f). The live check's one miss, ENG150 Logical Fallacies with 1 `mc_reason` item in 1 of 16 passes, is not accepted as a rate. After a generation call is written, a concept of that call that ends it with fewer than `CHECK_ITEM_MC_MIN_PER_CONCEPT` (2) stored `mc_reason` items gets `CHECK_ITEM_MC_TOPUP_CALLS` (1 †) focused call. The call asks for `mc_reason` items only, for that one concept, exactly the missing count, at the difficulties the concept lacks. It is shown the concept's own ranked passages and told why each of its dropped `mc_reason` drafts failed validation. Its drafts go through the same `repair_draft` and `validate_draft`, its write through the same A23 source re-checks, and at most the missing count is stored. No rule is relaxed. The call is billed to `llm_usage` (feature `check_items_topup`, task `check_items`, a run that fails after billing included) and counted by the new event `learn.check_items_topup` (usage; ids and counts only). A concept still below after its top-up is logged at WARNING with every drop reason of the call and the top-up.

The top-up is its own agent in its own module, `agents/check_items_topup.py`, with one prompt stack, on the `check_items` model slot. Its system prompt is built from the `check_items` prompt's rules, now named fragments; `_PROMPT` is byte-identical (hash `4710781c2f16`, pinned), so the eval replays at baseline. `draft_items` and the top-up share one run loop (`run_drafting`: Flex retries, never raises, bills what it cost). The E2E handler now drafts two `mc_reason` items per concept, so a function-mode pass needs no top-up.

Review of the ruling (commits 7ee8a56, 2bfba37, 3082c2c, c17b8c0, 73c88db). The `deliberation` rule read ": I will …" in A37's own closing sentence as the model's working. So an `mc_reason` item whose correct option is a first-person sentence always dropped: a language item's "I will visit Paris", the quotation "I think, therefore I am", the contraction "Let's". Its top-up could never fill it, since the fed-back reason asks the model to change the answer. A match whose words lie wholly inside a verbatim quote of the final answer or of an option's text (whole words, case and whitespace aside, closing punctuation dropped) is now not working (`checks._working`). Only a quote that holds the whole match hides it, so an option "I" never hides "So I think", and working beside a quote is still found. Replaying the recorded `mc_reason` references (244 unique across the lane's scratch records, 216 of them in the current schema) changes no verdict; the eval replays at baseline. The top-up's count is now the one read after the pass's write. It used to add the pass's upserted ids to that read, so a row retired in between (a document deleted or opted out meanwhile) still counted: the top-up asked for too few, the event over-reported, and the below-floor WARNING could be skipped. The read now returns every stored item of the concept (`_stored_items`: id, format and difficulty, all plaintext). A top-up draft whose prompt a stored item already has is a repeat. It is dropped before the missing count counts it (`_store_drafts(exclude_ids=)`), logged at INFO, and fed into the drop reasons as `repeat`. Before, it upserted onto the stored row by id: it took a new draft's slot, rewrote the stored item's content and difficulty (a free item became `mc_reason`), and was reported stored. A test now pins that a concept at `CHECK_ITEM_INITIAL_PER_CONCEPT` items gets no call and no top-up, whatever its `mc_reason` count (Known gaps, Open questions). The no-shared-passage backfill test asserted that `create_items` was not called, which a run never calls since 8ea9042. It now asserts that `_store_drafts` (every write of a run) and `draft_mc_topup` were not called and that nothing was upserted. The drafting-pool comment now bounds an upload at 4 generation runs plus 10 top-up runs on one thread, and spec §2's module map names `agents/check_items_topup.py`.

## Constants chosen

- `_SLUG_MAX_WORDS = 6` † and `_SLUG_DIGEST_LEN = 8` † (`learning/checks.py`): key-format literals, not loop tuning knobs, so they are not in `learning/params.py` (the `_MIN_TOKEN_LEN` precedent).
- `_NEGATIONS` and `_NO_MISCONCEPTION` (`learning/checks.py`, review of round 4): word lists, not tuning knobs. `_NEGATIONS` is exactly the negations that `_STOPWORDS` already lists. `_NO_MISCONCEPTION` holds "n/a", "na", "none", "null", "nil", "nothing", "tbd", "todo", "unknown" and "not applicable", compared as option texts are.
- `CHECK_ITEM_MC_MIN_PER_CONCEPT = 2` and `CHECK_ITEM_MC_TOPUP_CALLS = 1` † (`learning/params.py`, coordinator's ruling): the floor is the requirement "≥ 2 `mc_reason` stored per concept per pass", and it fits inside `CHECK_ITEM_DIFFICULTIES`, so a top-up can always ask for a difficulty the concept lacks. The dagger is as ruled.
- Unchanged: `CHECK_ITEM_MC_OPTIONS = 4` †, `CHECK_ITEM_FINAL_ANSWER_MAX_TOKENS = 20` †, `CHECK_ITEM_MIN_WRONG`.

## Known gaps

- Two distractors stating the same misconception still drop the item, as the decision says, and a deliberating reference still drops it. On the round-4 draft this was 6 of 432 `mc_reason` drafts in 72 direct calls, every one of them that case ("This crop moved from the Americas to Afro-Eurasia." on all three HIST200 distractors, "Confuses the recursive case with the base case." on two CS101 ones). A distractor stating no misconception drops too: a placeholder such as "N/A" counts as none, and so does such a free or teachback wrong text. Since the coordinator's ruling, a concept these drops leave below 2 `mc_reason` items gets one top-up call instead of staying there. In 40 direct top-up calls on the recorded misses, each fed its recorded reasons, 35 filled their request and 51 of the 56 items asked were stored. The misses were 3 final answers over 20 tokens and 2 items whose distractors again stated one misconception. One of those reused the kebab-case key its fed-back reason quoted. In the live check's forced pass, 1 of 8 top-ups (HIST200 Columbian Exchange) failed the same way.
- A37 known limit (1) remains at about the round-3 rate. A call that writes no "Final answer:" sentence on its free and teachback references loses them: 6 of 32 direct calls on CS101, 0 of 40 on ENG150, HIST200 and CHEM121. The round-3 draft had 8 of 44 and 1 of 24. This is open for the coordinator (spec §13 A37 (1)). The round-4 live check (d28fe4a) hit it once in 16 passes, in run 1's CS101 pass 2, which kept 1 of 12 free and teachback drafts. The live check on ff6fd3f hit it once in 16 passes too, in run 1's CS101 pass 1, which kept 0 of 12 (11 without the sentence, 1 with its final answer in the prompt).
- Requirement "≥ 2 `mc_reason` stored per concept per pass": met in every pass of the live check on the final code, 16 of 16 across two fresh-DB runs (12 of 12 for the three required subjects). No concept fell below 2 in those passes, so no top-up fired on its own; a labelled forced pass made 8 real ones (Live check, below). A residual remains when both the main call and its one top-up miss. That rate is not measured directly: a concept fell below 2 in 3 of 120 direct calls on the round-4 prompt (about 1 concept in 80), and a top-up misses about 1 in 8, so it is roughly 1 concept-pass in several hundred. Such a concept is logged at WARNING with every drop reason. A later generation run or the nightly backfill drafts it again only while it has fewer than `CHECK_ITEM_INITIAL_PER_CONCEPT` (9) items, because A23's skip counts items, not formats. A concept that ends a pass with 9 or more items but fewer than 2 `mc_reason` ones is never re-drafted for them. That needs two misses in a row on a partly covered concept (HANDOFF-04's partial-coverage gap), and no run has shown it. ## Open questions for the series owner

- A37 known limit (1): a call that leaves "Final answer:" off every free and teachback reference loses those drafts. A34 gives the free and teachback formats no second statement of the answer to repair against. Taken option: the prompt's closing-sentence lines, measured above; a structural fix (for example code closing every reference from the `final_answer` field) would change A34 and is the coordinator's call.
- Should a concept at `CHECK_ITEM_INITIAL_PER_CONCEPT` items or more with fewer than `CHECK_ITEM_MC_MIN_PER_CONCEPT` stored `mc_reason` items get a top-up-only pass on a later run? The ruling says a concept left below "is re-drafted by the next generation run / backfill", but A23 skips a concept at 9 items before any agent call, counting items, not formats. So such a concept stays below for good (the review of the ruling found this twice). Taken option: no call, pinned by a test, because a top-up there changes A23 ("a re-run costs nothing") and, without A38's redraft limit, would make every later upload and nightly backfill pay one call for a concept the model keeps missing. The alternative is cheap to build: when the A23 count is at 9, one plaintext `_stored_items` read, and if `mc_reason` is below 2, the same single top-up, still one per concept per pass. A38's limit would then bound it.
- Should a derived key reuse a key the course already uses for the same misconception text, so that PKG-10's rollup does not split it? That would need a read across items at write time. Taken option: derive from the text alone, deterministically.
- Resolved by the coordinator's ruling (2026-09-28, option (c)): a same-category `mc_reason` question ("which of these is a fallacy?" against three appeals) has distractors that genuinely share one misconception, and the decision rejects it.
